Check against the brief before implementation. Replace the generated system's marketing feature area with a song-specific example that combines topic, genre, tempo and arrangement in one place. That gives writers a concrete start and preserves their own draft until they choose to load it. Keep the three actual workflow stages legible and reserve green for actions and progress. Match labels and dates to the master text scale. Apply the same navigation and tokens to both routes, retain all existing provider and song-spec paths, and verify both layouts at 390px and 1440px.
